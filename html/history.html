History of Hydration

Way back when humans were hunter gatheres, some 50,000 years ago, humans were constantly on the move. It's hard to tell human anotomy back then, but the water was most likely disgusting. And humans didn't really have much of a risk to drink water born diseases.

About 10,000 years ago humans adopted what is known as agrarian lifestyle. All this means is that humans began to live in permanent settlements. This stationary way of living is what caused humans to be so dependant on water. It was at this point that diseases from dirty water became such a health risk to humans. What this meant is that if a urban area were to come about, clean water would have to be abundant ther first.
